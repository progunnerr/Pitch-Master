Wood1, Traditional, and Drums preset samples and loops

These are original procedural audio assets generated by scripts/generate-metronome.mjs.
They contain no third-party recordings and no Pro Metronome audio.

To the extent possible under law, the authors dedicate these generated audio assets
to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Format: 44,100 Hz, mono, 16-bit PCM WAV. Regular clicks are energy-matched across
presets. Wood1 uses hollow block resonances; Traditional uses a dry mechanical
tick with a ringing metal bell on beat 1; Drums uses a rim/cross-stick pulse with
a stronger snare strike on beat 1 and softer closed-hat subdivision taps.
Previews play one complete four-beat bar, including the accent and regular beats.
Loops are rendered at 80 BPM and played at 0.5–2x for the supported 40–160 BPM range.
Regenerate with: node scripts/generate-metronome.mjs
